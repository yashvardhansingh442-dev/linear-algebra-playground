# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

This project exists to help people learn linear algebra, from absolute beginners to people building serious numerical code. Learning means asking questions, making mistakes, and being corrected. We want everyone to feel safe doing all three.

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment:**

- Being patient with beginners. "Basic" questions are welcome here.
- Explaining *why*, not just giving the answer.
- Giving feedback that is specific, kind, and focused on the work, not the person.
- Accepting correction gracefully and crediting people who help you.
- Respecting different backgrounds, learning speeds, and approaches to the math.
- Citing sources and giving credit for ideas, explanations, and code that are not your own.
- Focusing on what is best for learners and for the community.

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Mocking, belittling, or talking down to someone for not knowing something ("this is trivial", "just read the textbook").
- Gatekeeping, or implying that someone doesn't belong in math or programming.
- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberately disruptive behavior, or spamming issues and pull requests.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Submitting copied material (lecture notes, textbook text, problem set solutions) without permission or credit, or posting solutions to graded coursework in a way that enables cheating.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational setting.

## Community Guidelines for Learning Content

- **Corrections are contributions.** If you find a mistake in an explanation, proof, or code, please open an issue or pull request. Be kind: assume the author made an honest error.
- **Disagreements about math are resolved with arguments and examples**, not authority. Counterexamples are always welcome.
- **Questions are not spam.** If an explanation is unclear, say so using the "explanation unclear" issue template. That feedback improves the material for everyone.
- **AI-generated content** may be used to help, but contributors are responsible for checking its correctness and must disclose substantial AI assistance in their pull request.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official channels. It also applies when an individual is representing the project or its community in public spaces, such as using an official project email address or acting as a designated representative at an online or in-person event.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior, and will take fair and appropriate corrective action in response to any behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

Please include, where possible: what happened, where it happened (links help), when, and anyone involved or who witnessed it.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in deciding the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior considered unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning with clarity about the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the project for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project.

## Appeals

If you believe an enforcement decision was made in error, you may reply to the maintainer's message with your reasoning. Appeals will be reviewed, though maintainers' decisions are final.
